01 / ABOUT

先理解结构，再追求收益。Understand structure.
Then pursue returns.

一份简历之外的自我介绍：我喜欢把模糊的交易直觉拆成假设、数据、约束和可复现的结果。

A little beyond the resume: I like turning fuzzy trading intuition into explicit hypotheses, data, constraints and reproducible results.

  • 中频因子与订单流Medium-frequency factors & order flow
  • 商品 / 股指期货策略Commodity & index futures strategies
  • 回测、成本与风险控制Backtests, costs & risk controls
  • 稳定可观测的交易系统Observable, resilient trading systems

我的工作常常从盘口快照和分钟级行情开始：先定义成交口径，再处理聚合、平滑、去极值与对齐，最后在交易成本、换月、整数手数和执行延迟等现实约束下检验信号。

My work often starts with depth snapshots and minute-level market data: define execution assumptions first, then handle aggregation, smoothing, outlier control and alignment, and finally test signals under real constraints such as costs, rollovers, integer sizing and latency.

我也关心研究如何真正落地——从模型输出到资金分配、母单发送、持仓核验、告警和故障熔断，每一个环节都要能解释、能恢复、能复盘。

I also care about the last mile: from model outputs to capital allocation, parent-order submission, reconciliation, alerting and fail-closed safeguards. Every link should be explainable, recoverable and reviewable.
